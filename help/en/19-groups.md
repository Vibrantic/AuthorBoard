# Groups

Groups bundle notes into a shared frame on the board – useful for scene clusters, relationship diagrams, or anything you want to **move together** without locking it.

## Creating a group

1. **Select** the notes you want in the group (rubber-band on the empty board, Shift+click or Ctrl+click).
2. Press **Ctrl+G** – or **right-click the board → Group from selection**.
3. A frame appears around the notes, with a small margin.

You can add more notes later by **dragging them into the frame**, unless you locked the group.

## Editing a group

- **Drag the frame edge**: the whole group moves with its notes.
- **Right-click the frame → Rename group …**: the group's title.
- **Group color**: presets, custom color or default – colors the frame, not the notes.
- **Bring group to front**: brings only the frame in front of the notes.
- **Lock group / Unlock group**: a locked group cannot be moved; the notes inside can still be edited individually.
- **Ungroup**: the frame disappears, the notes stay.
- **Delete group with notes**: deletes the frame and every note inside (asks first).

## Groups and focus mode

Focus mode applies to groups too: when a note inside a group is selected, only **its own** connections stay visible – not the whole group. To see the group together, select all its notes (right-click the frame → *Select notes in this group*, if the menu offers it) or Shift+click each.

## Groups and the notes list

The notes list (left dock) does **not** list groups as an entry – only the notes inside them. To quickly find a group, give it a name and search for the group title (the search also covers group titles when groups exist in the notebook).

## Limits

- A note can belong to **one** group at a time.
- Groups are purely visual: they don't change note data, connections or exports – they only help organize the board.
