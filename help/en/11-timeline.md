# Timeline

Open it with **View → Timeline**. It shows every note that has the attribute **Date** on a horizontal time axis.

## Dates

| Format | Example |
|---|---|
| Day.Month.Year | `18.11.2004` |
| Year-Month-Day | `2004-11-18` |
| Month.Year | `07.1195` |
| Year | `1187` |

Add the attribute **Time** (`14:30`) to order events within a day. Years can be fictional (`1203`) or negative.

## Using the timeline

- Each note is a **dot in the colour of the note**. Dots that lie close together are stacked.
- **Click a dot** to open an information bubble with the title, type and date.
  - **›** jumps to the note on the board.
  - **Always show label** keeps a small label next to the dot.
- **Mouse wheel** zooms, **dragging** moves the axis, **double-click** the empty area shows everything. The axis switches between years and months depending on the zoom.
- Notes without a date are counted at the bottom right.
- Hover a dot for a tooltip.
