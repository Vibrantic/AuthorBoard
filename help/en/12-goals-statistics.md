# Goals and statistics

## Word counts

The status bar shows the number of notes and the words in all note texts. The count per note appears in the Inspector and in the editor.

## Word goal

Set a **word goal** and a **deadline** in the notebook info. The shelf spine shows a green progress bar, and the status bar shows *Goal x %*.

## Statistics (Ctrl+I)

*View → Statistics …* summarises the notebook:

- notes, words, threads, average words per note
- progress towards the word goal, words still to write, deadline and **words written today**
- notes and words per note type
- status distribution and the most frequent tags
- the longest notes and the most connected notes
