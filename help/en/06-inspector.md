# The Inspector

The Inspector (right dock, tab **Inspector**) edits the selected note. Its width never changes, so the board does not shift when you select a note.

## Sections

- **Note type and colour** - changing the type adds the suggested attributes and, as long as you did not choose a custom colour, switches to the type's colour.
- **Status** - *Draft*, *Revise*, *Done*; shown as a coloured dot on the note and usable as a filter.
- **Lock position** - locked notes cannot be moved or resized.
- **Title**
- **Tags** - separate with commas. While you type, **suggestions** appear from all tags you used before in this book. Press Enter or Tab to accept. Tags are remembered per notebook.
- **Attributes** - free name/value pairs such as *Date*, *Time* or *Place*. Use **＋ Attribute** for suggestions or your own name. Drag the dotted bar below the table to **make it taller or shorter**.
- **Content** - the text of the note, with spell checking, links and comments. Open the [full-size editor](topic:07-editor) for formatting.
- **Attachments** - see below.
- **Connections** - see below.

## Attachments

Attach any file or image to a note: **＋ Attachment** or drag files onto the list. Images show a thumbnail. **Double-click** an attachment to open it with your default program. Select and press *Remove* to detach it. Files up to 40 MB are accepted. The note shows a paperclip with the number of attachments.

## Connections

Lists the notes connected by **threads** and the notes that **mention each other** by title in their text (`←` mentioned in, `→` mentions). Double-click to jump to a note.

- Type in the **search field** to filter by title or thread label.
- Use the **kind filter** (All / Threads / mentioned in / mentions) and the **type filter** to narrow the list.
- Drag the dotted bar under the list to **scale** it.
