# The bookshelf

The start page shows your library as a bookshelf. Each **notebook** is a book spine, each **shelf** is a *category* such as "Novels" or "Short stories".

## Books

- The **thickness** of a spine grows with the word count of the notebook.
- A **green bar** at the foot of the spine shows the progress towards the word goal (see [Notebooks](topic:03-notebooks)).
- **Double-click** a book to open it. Hover it to see author, genre, notes, words, goal and deadline.
- **Drag** a book to another shelf or to another position on the same shelf.
- **Right-click** a book: Open, Edit info, Book colour, Move to category, Duplicate, Delete.

## Shelves (categories)

- **Create** one with **＋ Category**. **Rename** it by double-clicking its title.
- **Drag the shelf title** up or down to change the order of the shelves - the books move with the shelf.
- **Right-click the title**: Rename, New notebook here, **Shelf colour** (presets, custom colour or default), Move up / down, Delete.
- Deleting a shelf moves its books to another shelf. The last shelf cannot be deleted.

## Search and import

- The search box above the shelves dims all books that do not match title, author, genre, description or custom fields.
- **Import …** copies existing board files (`.json`) into the library. If the file contains book information (title, author, genre, word goal …) it is applied.
- The dotted **＋** slot at the end of each shelf creates a new notebook in that category.

Your library is stored in the folder `~/.authorboard/` (see [Troubleshooting](topic:17-troubleshooting)).
