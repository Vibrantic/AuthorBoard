# Working on the board

## Navigating

| Action | How |
|---|---|
| Zoom | Mouse wheel (zooms towards the pointer) |
| Pan | Hold the **middle mouse button** and drag |
| Select several notes | Drag a rectangle on the empty board |
| Show everything | **Ctrl+0** (*Show all*), 100 % zoom: Ctrl+1 |

## Notes

- **Create:** double-click the empty board, press **Shift+Space** (note type search), use **＋ Note** in the toolbar, or right-click the board → *New note here*.
- **Move** by dragging, **resize** by dragging the folded lower-right corner.
- **Duplicate** with Ctrl+D (copies text, attributes, pins, attachments and comments, but no threads).
- **Delete** with the Delete key or the context menu.
- Hover a note for a summary: title, type, attributes, tags and word count.
- *Snap to grid* (View menu) makes notes snap while you move them. *Arrange by note type* sorts notes into columns (locked notes stay where they are).

## Pins

- **Double-click a note** to add a pin at that spot. Drag a pin to move it inside its note.
- **Right-click a pin**: change its colour, remove its threads, **lock / unlock** it or delete it.
- A **locked pin** cannot be dragged inside the note (shown with a small keyhole) but **still moves together with its note**.

## Threads

- **Connect two pins:** press and hold the **right mouse button** on a pin, drag to another pin and release.
- **Right-click a thread:** colour, label (for example "brother of") and delete.
- Threads lie **in front of the notes**. Where a thread crosses a note it is drawn fainter so the text stays readable. The pin heads always stay on top.

## Focus mode

When you select a note, notes and threads that are **not connected** to it are greyed out. See [Locking and focus mode](topic:09-locking-focus).
